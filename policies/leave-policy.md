# Leave of Absence Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002
**Applies to:** All employees in Canadian operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-02-03

## 1. Scope

This policy covers medical leave, parental leave, bereavement leave, and unpaid personal leave.
The effective date referenced throughout this document is the first day of the requested leave.

## 2. Leave request review

All leave requests are reviewed by HR before approval. Requests that meet any of the criteria in
Clause 2.2 require additional review and must not be auto-approved.

> **Clause 2.1** — A submitted leave request must be reviewed and flagged for HR attention within
> 2 days of the leave request being submitted.

> **Clause 2.2** — A leave request must be flagged for additional review where the requested
> duration exceeds 15 consecutive working days, where the request is retroactive, or where the
> employee has taken more than 20 days of leave in the preceding 12 months.

Action: `flag_leave_request`

## 3. Medical leave

> **Clause 3.1** — Medical leave exceeding 5 consecutive working days requires a medical
> certificate. The certificate must be requested within 2 days of the leave start date.

> **Clause 3.2** — Medical leave is job-protected for up to 26 weeks in a 52-week period.

## 4. Return to work

> **Clause 4.1** — A return-to-work check must be scheduled within 10 days of the leave start
> date for any leave expected to exceed 10 consecutive working days.

> **Clause 4.2** — For medical leave, the return-to-work check must confirm any accommodation
> requirements before the employee resumes duties.

Action: `schedule_return_to_work_check`

## 5. Parental leave

> **Clause 5.1** — In Quebec, parental leave is administered under the Quebec Parental Insurance
> Plan (QPIP). Employees must be directed to QPIP enrolment within 10 days of the leave start
> date.

> **Clause 5.2** — Outside Quebec, parental leave benefits are administered through Employment
> Insurance. Standard return-to-work scheduling under Clause 4.1 applies.

## 6. Benefits during leave

> **Clause 6.1** — Group benefit coverage continues unchanged for the first 90 days of any
> approved leave.

> **Clause 6.2** — Where a leave is expected to exceed 90 days, a benefits review must be
> scheduled within 30 days of the leave start date to determine premium-sharing arrangements
> for the remainder of the leave.

Action: `schedule_benefits_review`

## 7. Bereavement leave

Bereavement leave of up to 5 days is granted without a formal review requirement. No action is
triggered under Section 2 for bereavement leave of 5 days or fewer.
